Around the World in Eighty Days

by Jules Verne

Published in 1872

Rating: 3/5

  • adventure
  • travel

Phileas Fogg bets half his fortune that he can circle the globe in eighty days, and sets off from London the same evening with his new valet, Passepartout.

Why read it

A race against the calendar told with dry humour, where every steamer, train and elephant counts.

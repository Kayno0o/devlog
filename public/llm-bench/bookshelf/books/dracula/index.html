Dracula

by Bram Stoker

Published in 1897

Rating: 4/5

  • horror
  • gothic

A young solicitor travels to Transylvania to close a property sale with a count who never seems to eat, and comes back with a story nobody in London wants to believe.

Why read it

Told entirely through letters and diaries, so the reader always knows a little more than each narrator.
